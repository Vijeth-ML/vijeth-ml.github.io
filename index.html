01 / THE METHOD

Every project starts
with a question.

Not a list of technologies. A problem worth understanding. The work is the journey from uncertainty to evidence—and knowing what remains unsolved.

01

Identify the signal

What is the real-world problem? What makes it difficult, noisy, ambiguous or important?

02

Build an experiment

Choose methods deliberately. Establish baselines, test assumptions and compare alternatives.

03

Interrogate the evidence

Use metrics and visualisations to understand what worked, what failed and where bias can hide.

04

Find the next question

Document limitations and turn them into a credible path for further investigation.

03 / NEXT FRONTIER

Looking for worlds
hidden in the data.

My research direction is robust machine learning for astronomy, with a particular interest in exoplanet detection from Kepler and TESS light curves.

  • Detect faint transit signals amid stellar variability and noise.
  • Distinguish planetary transits from eclipsing binaries and other false positives.
  • Study domain shift between instruments, missions and observing conditions.
  • Build models that generalise—and communicate uncertainty honestly.
