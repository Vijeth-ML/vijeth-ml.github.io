WHERE I’M HEADING

Research interests

My intended PhD direction is the use of robust machine learning for astronomical data, particularly exoplanet detection from Kepler and TESS light curves.

I’m interested in the practical questions behind a reliable detector: noise and stellar variability, class imbalance, false positives, calibration, and performance shifts between instruments or datasets.
